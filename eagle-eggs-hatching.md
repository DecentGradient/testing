# Eagle Eggs and Hatching

## Egg Characteristics
* **Clutch Size:** Typical clutch sizes are 1 to 3 eggs.
* **Laying Intervals:** Eagles typically lay an egg every 2 to 5 days until the clutch is complete.

## Incubation Period
* **Duration:** The incubation period averages 34 to 36 days for Bald Eagles, and 41 to 45 days for Golden Eagles.
* **Duties:** Both male and female eagles share incubation duties.

## Hatching Process
* **Asynchronous Hatching:** Eggs hatch asynchronously, often 2 to 4 days apart in the order they were laid.
* **Pipping and the Egg Tooth:** The eaglet uses a specialized sharp bump called an "egg tooth" to break through the shell. The first crack they make is called a "pip".
* **Duration:** Breaking free from the shell is an exhausting process that takes 24 to 48 hours to complete.

## Early Life
* **At Hatching:** Eaglets are born in an altricial state (blind and completely helpless), weighing roughly 3 ounces.
* **Fledging:** The fledging timelines are typically between 10 to 12 weeks of age.
