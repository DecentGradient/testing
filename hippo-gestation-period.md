# Hippopotamus Gestation Period and Birth Facts

## Gestation Period
The typical length of pregnancy for a hippopotamus is approximately 8 months, or about 227 to 240 days.

## Birth Details
Mothers often give birth to a single calf in isolation. This event frequently happens entirely underwater, though it can occur on land.

## Birth Weight
Newborn hippos are quite large, weighing between 50 and 121 pounds (25 to 55 kilograms) at birth.

## Underwater Behaviors
Despite being born in the water, newborn hippos can only hold their breath for about 40 seconds. However, calves have the remarkable ability to nurse underwater; they will instinctively detach, pop up to the surface for a quick breath, and then sink back down to continue nursing.
