# History of Cancer Treatments

## Introduction
* Brief overview of oncology across history.
* Old-world perspectives on cancer (ancient civilizations, Greek and Roman theories of humors).
* Transition from a perceived death sentence to treatable conditions.

## Late 19th - Early 20th Century
* **Birth of Anesthesia and Antisepsis** (1846 & 1867): Revolutionizing medicine and allowing surgical access to internal tumors.
* **Radical Mastectomy** (1882): Dr. William Halsted's aggressive surgical intervention for breast cancer.
* **Early Immunotherapy / Coley's Toxins** (1891): Dr. William Coley's bacterial injections for bone tumors.
* **Discovery of X-rays** (1895): Wilhelm Röntgen's discovery and early testing of radiation oncology.
* **Radium and Polonium** (1898): Pierre and Marie Curie's discovery of radioactivity for localized brachytherapy.

## 1940s - 1970s
* **Hormone-Dependent Cancers** (1941): Dr. Charles Huggins' discovery of hormonal therapies for prostate cancer.
* **First Chemotherapy** (1942/1946): Nitrogen mustard (derived from chemical warfare research on mustard gas) by Goodman and Gilman.
* **Anti-Metabolites for Leukemia** (1947): Dr. Sidney Farber's use of aminopterin to treat pediatric leukemia.
* **First Cure of a Solid Tumor** (1956): Methotrexate chemotherapy for metastatic solid tumors by Hertz and Li.
* **Bone Marrow Transplantation** (1950s/1960s): Dr. E. Donnall Thomas laying foundations for future immunotherapies.
* **Combination Chemotherapy** (1965): Vincent DeVita and colleagues proving that multi-drug regimens could cure Hodgkin's lymphoma.

## 1980s - 2000s
* **First Approved Immunotherapy** (1986): FDA approval of interferon-alpha 2.
* **First Monoclonal Antibody** (1997): FDA approval of Rituximab to mark cancer cells for immune destruction.
* **Target-Specific Therapy** (1998): FDA approval of Trastuzumab (Herceptin) for HER2-positive breast cancer.
* **First Small-Molecule Targeted Drug** (2001): FDA approval of Imatinib (Gleevec) targeting the BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase.
* **Anti-Angiogenesis** (2004): FDA approval of Bevacizumab (Avastin) to starve tumor blood supply.

## 2010s - Present
* **First Therapeutic Cancer Vaccine** (2010): FDA approval of Sipuleucel-T (Provenge).
* **Checkpoint Inhibitors Unleashed** (2011/2014): Ipilimumab (Yervoy), Pembrolizumab (Keytruda), and Nivolumab (Opdivo).
* **CAR T-Cell Therapy** (2017): Tisagenlecleucel (Kymriah), utilizing genetically reprogrammed patient T cells.
* **Nobel Prize Recognition** (2018): James P. Allison and Tasuku Honjo for discovery of cancer immunotherapy checkpoint inhibition.
* **TCR and Neoadjuvant Immunotherapy** (2022): Tebentafusp (Kimmtrak) approval and neoadjuvant clinical trials reducing recurrence.
