# Getting Rich: Fundamental Principles of Wealth Building

## Introduction
Building wealth is a journey that requires discipline, education, and consistent effort. This guide outlines the core principles to help you achieve financial independence.

## Budgeting & Saving
Living below your means is the cornerstone of wealth creation. Track your expenses, create a realistic budget, and aim to save a significant percentage of your income. Emergency funds are essential.

## Investing
Saving alone won't make you rich; investing will. Understand the power of compound interest. Diversify your assets across index funds, stocks, and real estate to mitigate risk while capturing long-term market growth.

## Increasing Income
Your income is your greatest wealth-building tool. Invest in yourself to develop high-income skills, seek career advancement, or start a side hustle. Increasing your earning potential accelerates your journey.

## Patience & Consistency
Wealth building is a marathon, not a sprint. Stay the course, avoid get-rich-quick schemes, and consistently apply these principles over years and decades.
