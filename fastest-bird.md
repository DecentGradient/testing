# Fastest Birds

Here is some information about the fastest birds on Earth:

- **Peregrine Falcon**: Recognized as the fastest bird (and animal) on Earth, capable of reaching speeds over 240 mph (386 km/h) during its characteristic hunting stoop (dive).
- **Golden Eagle**: Second fastest bird, reaching speeds up to 150-200 mph (241-320 km/h) in a dive.
- **White-throated Needletail**: Known for having the fastest level (horizontal) flight speed among birds, reaching up to 105 mph (169 km/h).
- **Common Swift**: Highly agile bird capable of reaching speeds up to 70 mph (112 km/h) in horizontal flight.
