# Fastest Swimming Fish

- **Black Marlin:** Up to 80-82 mph (129 km/h). Often cited in fishing lore as the fastest, though recent science suggests sustained speeds are lower.
- **Sailfish:** Up to 68 mph (110 km/h). Widely accepted as the undisputed fastest fish by reliable metrics.
- **Swordfish:** Up to 60 mph (97 km/h). Possesses a special oil-secreting gland to reduce friction drag.
- **Shortfin Mako Shark:** Up to 45-60 mph (72-97 km/h). The fastest shark in the ocean.
- **Wahoo:** Up to 48-50 mph (77-80 km/h). Nicknamed the "Texas Torpedo".
- **Anatomy Facts:** Note that these fish share hydrodynamic torpedo-shaped bodies, crescent-shaped tails, and fin retraction for drag reduction.
