# Rhino Gestation Period and Birth Facts

## Gestation Period
The gestation period of a rhinoceros is among the longest of any land mammal, lasting anywhere from 15 to 18 months, depending on the species.
* **White Rhinos**: 16 to 18 months
* **Black Rhinos**: 15 to 16 months
* **Indian, Javan, and Sumatran Rhinos**: 15 to 16 months

## Birth Facts
* **Single Calves**: A rhino mother almost always gives birth to a single calf.
* **Weight**: Newborns typically weigh between 80 and 150 lbs at birth.
* **Born Hornless**: Rhino calves are born completely without horns for the mother's safety during birth.
* **Mobility**: Calves can stand up and walk within just an hour of being born.

## Early Life & Motherhood
* **Growth**: Rhino calves pack on size incredibly fast, gaining between 2 and 3 lbs a day during their first year of life.
* **Nursing**: They nurse for up to two years.
* **Independence**: Calves remain highly dependent on their mother for protection, staying close by her side for 2 to 4 years.
