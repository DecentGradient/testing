# Horse Gestation Period

- **Gestation Period:** Approximately 11 to 11.5 months (about 330-345 days).
- **Birth Facts:** Typically a single foal is born. Twins are rare and often present complications.
- **Newborn Stats:** A healthy foal usually weighs about 10% of the mare's body weight.
- **Early Mobility:** Foals are precocial, meaning they can stand within 1-2 hours of birth and run shortly after. 
- **Early Life:** Foals begin nursing within a few hours of birth and are usually weaned around 4-6 months of age.
